EVE Online Wormhole Classes & Statics Explained

C1-C6, frigate holes, Thera and shattered systems, effects, and how statics work.

A Nexum wormhole guide

Wormhole space - "J-space" - is the ~2,600 unknown systems that only connect through wormholes, never gates. Each system has a class that sets its difficulty and rewards, sometimes a system effect that buffs or nerfs your ships, and a static that decides where it reliably connects. Understanding these three things is most of what you need to live in, or roam through, wormholes.

The wormhole classes (C1-C6)

The main classes scale in Sleeper NPC difficulty, loot, and - importantly for movement - wormhole mass allowances.

ClassFeelNotes
C1StarterLow ship-mass holes; cruiser-friendly. Often has a highsec static.
C2CasualTwo statics is common; a frequent choice for small corps.
C3Solid ISKPopular home class; strong ratting for the risk, usually a k-space static.
C4DefensiveDual wormhole statics and no direct k-space static - hard to find, hard to invade.
C5CapitalHigh mass holes; capital ratting and escalations. Serious PvP.
C6EndgameMaximum mass and reward; large-scale capital brawls.

Special classes

  • C13 - frigate-sized "shattered" holes; only frigates, destroyers and a few small hulls fit through the tiny mass limits.
  • Shattered systems - no planets you can anchor on, often multiple statics and a static effect; used for daytripping and hunting.
  • Thera - a unique, large shattered system with many static exits all over New Eden. It is a hub for explorers and roamers and is heavily patrolled.

System effects

Many wormhole systems have a persistent effect that changes your ship stats - and the strength scales with class (a C6 effect is far stronger than a C1). The main ones:

  • Pulsar - boosts shields, reduces armour resists, cuts capacitor recharge.
  • Magnetar - boosts damage and drone stats but hurts tracking, missile explosion and targeting.
  • Black Hole - boosts speed, range and missile velocity; hurts tracking and inertia.
  • Red Giant - boosts overheat, smartbombs, bombs and precision.
  • Cataclysmic Variable - boosts remote reps and capacitor capacity; reduces recharge and local rep.
  • Wolf-Rayet - big boost to armour resists and small weapon damage; reduces shield HP - a small-ship brawler's effect.

Always check a system's effect before you commit a fight in it - a Magnetar can double a brawler's damage, and a Pulsar can flip a fit's tank on its head.

Statics vs wandering connections

A static is a connection a system always regenerates to a fixed destination class. When a system's static dies (collapsed or expired), a new one of the same type spawns somewhere else. So a C3 with a highsec static will always have a route to highsec - the specific highsec system changes, the guarantee does not. Statics are why corps pick a home class: a reliable highsec static makes logistics easy; a C5 static makes for constant capital content.

Wandering connections are the random, temporary holes that open into your system from elsewhere. They are unpredictable and are the usual way roamers and invaders arrive - which is why you scan your home every day.

K162 - the exit side

Every wormhole has two ends. The side that was opened from the other system shows up as a K162 and carries no mass/type info of its own. To know the real hole type, mass and lifetime, you read the named signature (e.g. "N062", "C247") on the opposite side. A mapper resolves this for you automatically from your scan results.
