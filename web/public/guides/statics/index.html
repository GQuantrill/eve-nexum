EVE Online Wormhole Types & Statics List

Every wormhole code - where it leads, whether it's static, and its mass and lifetime.

A Nexum wormhole reference

Every wormhole in EVE has a type code - like N062, C247 or O477 - and that code always tells you the same three things: where it leads, the biggest ship that fits, and how much mass it can take before it collapses. This page is the full lookup table, plus a short primer on statics so the columns make sense.

Statics vs wandering connections

A static is a connection a system always regenerates to a fixed destination class. When a system's static dies - collapsed or expired - a new one of the same type spawns somewhere else. So a C3 with a highsec static always has a route to highsec; the specific highsec system changes, the guarantee does not. That is why corps choose a home by its static: a highsec static makes logistics easy, a C5 static means constant capital content. A wandering connection, by contrast, is random and temporary - a hole that happened to open into your system from somewhere else. A K162 is always the exit side of a hole opened elsewhere; jump through and read the named signature on the far side to identify what it really is. For what the classes themselves mean, see wormhole classes explained.

How to read the table

  • Type - the wormhole's code, shown on the named side of the hole.
  • Respawn - whether the hole appears as a static (guaranteed, regenerating), a wandering connection, or the reverse (K162) side.
  • Spawns in - the space where the named side is found.
  • Leads to - the class of space on the other end.
  • Max ship - the largest hull that can jump the hole (per-jump mass limit).
  • Total mass - the mass budget before the hole collapses (what you deplete when rolling).
  • Lifetime - how long the hole lives before it expires on its own.
